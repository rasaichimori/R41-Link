man midi-mapping.md
  • Display: 26×27 = 13×9 blocks of 2×3 pixels.
  • Notes 11–127: block index = 127 − note (top-left = 127).
  • Velocity 1–63 selects one of 64 patterns inside each block (6-bit glyph).
  • Still images export one chord of note-ons; video exports a frame range at your chosen FPS with timing from BPM.
